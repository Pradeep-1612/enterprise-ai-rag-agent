Certificate Management Policy

Production SSL/TLS certificates must be renewed before their
expiration date.

Certificates expiring within 30 days are considered at risk.

Certificate renewal requires validating the certificate,
checking the associated trading partner connection, and
deploying the renewed certificate.

The operations team should be notified when a certificate
has fewer than 15 days remaining.

Emergency renewal is required when a production certificate
has fewer than 7 days remaining.
